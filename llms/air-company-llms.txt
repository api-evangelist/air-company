# Air Company (AIRCO)

> Air Company, which operates as AIRCO, is a carbon conversion technology company founded in 2017 by Gregory Constantine and Dr. Stafford Sheehan. Its Carbon Conversion Reactor combines captured CO2 and green hydrogen with renewable energy to produce carbon-negative alcohols and fully formulated synthetic fuels, sold and licensed under the AIRMADE brand. Air Company does not publish a public API, developer portal, or machine-readable API contract.

## API surface

- No public API. A contract-discovery pass on 2026-07-31 probed both site hosts and eight candidate API/docs subdomains for OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL, MCP, AsyncAPI, llms.txt and `/.well-known/*` documents; every probe returned HTTP 404 or NXDOMAIN. See `well-known/air-company-well-known.yml` for the recorded evidence.

## Products

- [AIRMADE SAF](https://www.aircompany.com/technology): Sustainable aviation fuel synthesized from CO2 and green hydrogen.
- [AIRMADE Ethanol / Methanol / Diesel / Rocket Fuel](https://www.aircompany.com/technology): Licensable catalytic process technologies for synthetic alcohols and fuels.
- [AIRCO MAD Fuel System](https://www.aircompany.com/technology): Modular, shipping-container-scale fuel production system.
- [AIRCO SAF Factory](https://www.aircompany.com/technology): Factory-scale synthetic aviation fuel production facility.
- [AIRCO Goods](https://www.aircompany.com/shop): Shopify-backed merchandise shop.
